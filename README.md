# ∫ Calculus Quest

A browser game that teaches **calculus from zero** using real-world stories and pictures, not formulas.
It works for **10–12 year olds** and for **engineering students**.

Play online: https://yashwanth9980.github.io/FirstRepo/. Or just open `index.html` in any browser (no install, works offline).

## Two modes
- **🧒 Explorer (age 10+)**: everything is explained with stories: skate ramps, cars, taps, cooling soup, a thrown ball, rockets. No formulas.
- **🎓 Engineer**: the same stories, plus the math notation and extra formula questions (power rule, limits, antiderivatives, the Fundamental Theorem).

## How it plays
Each world starts with a short **interactive story lesson** from Professor Owl (sliders, animations, things to try), then the questions. You get **3 hearts** per world. Finish a world to unlock the next and add a "big idea" to your notebook.

| World | Big idea | Real-world examples |
|---|---|---|
| ⛰️ Steepness Hills | Slope and rates | skate ramps, car vs bike race, tap flow, pocket money per week, trip graphs |
| 🔍 Getting Closer | Limits | walking halfway to a wall, cooling soup, sharing a pizza, a bridge with a missing plank |
| 🏎️ Speed Right Now | Derivative = speed at an instant | speedometer, zooming into a thrown ball's path, skier on a hill, roller coaster |
| 🥋 Pattern Dojo | Derivative shortcuts | ball rolling down a ramp, falling stone, train, lift |
| 🧮 Adding It Up | Integral = area = total | distance from speed, water in a tank, money earned, rocket rectangles |
| 🐉 Final Boss | Mixed review | 10 questions from every world |

Questions are randomly generated, so every replay is different. Each answer comes with an explanation, and the graph reveals the idea. You also get hints, XP, streaks and stars. Progress is saved in the browser.
